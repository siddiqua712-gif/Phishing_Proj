About Phishing Email Detector

This system detects phishing emails using machine learning. Users enter email subject, sender, and content to check whether the email is safe or phishing.

Steps to use Phishing E-mail detector:

  • Go to Phishing Email page from on the navigation bar
  • Enter your Email's subject, sender, body etc.
  • Click on "Check Email".
  • The system will show if it is a phishing email or a legitimate email.
  • Click "Clear" to enter a new Email.
